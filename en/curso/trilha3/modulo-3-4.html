✅ Getting Started Checklist

Before diving into the tools, make sure you have the necessary foundation to start implementing AI effectively.

📋 Essential Checklist

  • □Create accounts on ChatGPT and Claude (free versions)
  • □Try 10 different prompts in your day-to-day work
  • □Identify 3 repetitive tasks in your work
  • □Map 1 complete process for automation
  • □Create an account with a no-code tool (Zapier/Make)
  • □Set up 1 simple automation that works
  • □Share what you learned with 1 colleague

📄 Ready-to-Use Templates and Frameworks

Tested templates you can copy and adapt for your context immediately.

📝 Prompt Templates

  • •SWOT analysis: "Act as a strategic consultant. Perform a SWOT analysis of [company/project] in the [X] sector..."
  • •Cold email: "You are an experienced SDR. Create a personalized email for the [title] at [company] about [pain point]..."
  • •LinkedIn post: "Create a professional post about [topic] with a hook, value, and CTA. Tone: [authoritative/casual]..."
  • •Meeting summary: "Analyze this transcript and extract: decisions, action items, owners, and deadlines..."

💡 Practical Tip

Create a personal “prompt library.” Save your best prompts in a document organized by category. Refine them over time based on the results.

🎯 90-Day Plan

A practical guide to transform your work with AI in three months.

1-30

Month 1: Exploration

Use AI daily for at least 3 tasks. Try different tools. Document what works. Focus: build fluency.

31-60

Month 2: Automation

Automate 3-5 processes with no-code. Integrate AI into existing workflows. Measure time savings. Focus: productivity.

61-90

Month 3: Scale

Share with your team. Implement in larger projects. Document ROI. Focus: organizational impact.
