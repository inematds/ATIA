🔮 The Future of Work and Society

Trends, horizons, and the transformations that will shape the next decade.

📝 6 Topics ⏱️ ~30 min 🎯 Advanced
1

🌊 Trends 2025-2030

What Lies Ahead

The pace of AI’s evolution is unprecedented in the history of technology. What seemed like science fiction two years ago is already a reality. Autonomous agents, multimodal AI, increasingly capable language models, and the democratization of these tools are reshaping every sector of the economy. Understanding the trends taking shape for the next five years is essential to positioning yourself strategically.

Main Concept

The main trends for 2025-2030 include: (1) AI Agents - autonomous systems that perform complex tasks with minimal human supervision, combining reasoning, planning, and action; (2) Multimodal AI - models that process and generate text, images, audio, and video in an integrated way; (3) Small Language Models - smaller, more efficient models that run locally on devices; (4) Edge AI - AI processing on local devices instead of the cloud, for minimal latency and privacy; (5) Generative AI for code - increasingly capable coding copilots that democratize software development; (6) Extreme personalization - AI that adapts to individuals in healthcare, education, entertainment, and work.

📅 Technology Horizon

2025

AI agents become mainstream. Multimodal AI integrated into everyday products. First effective global regulations.

2026-27

AI embodied in advanced personal assistants. Automation of 40%+ of office tasks. Edge AI on every device.

2028-30

AI systems with advanced reasoning. Convergence with robotics and biotech. New job categories based entirely on AI.

💡 Practical Tip

Don't try to predict the future exactly — instead, build "antennas" to detect change. Subscribe to specialized newsletters (The Batch, AI Weekly), follow key researchers on Twitter/LinkedIn, join communities, and set aside 2 hours a week to try new AI tools.

🤖 AGI and Superintelligence

AI Horizons

AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) is the idea of an AI that matches or surpasses human intelligence across all cognitive tasks. Unlike today’s AI, which is "narrow" (excellent at specific tasks but unable to generalize), AGI would be able to learn anything, reason abstractly, and adapt to new situations. It’s one of the most debated and controversial topics in modern technology, with profound implications for humanity.

Main Concept

There are levels of AI: (1) Narrow AI (ANI) — what we have today, excellent at specific tasks; (2) Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) — equivalent to human intelligence in breadth; (3) Superintelligence (ASI) — surpassing human intelligence in every dimension. The AI Safety field works on the "alignment problem"—how to ensure that a superintelligent AI acts in accordance with human values and interests. Researchers such as Stuart Russell and organizations such as the AI Safety Institute investigate how to build AI that is fundamentally safe. Estimates of when (and whether) we will achieve AGI vary enormously: some researchers say 5-10 years, others say decades, and some argue that it may never happen with current approaches.

📊 Important Data

  • • In a 2024 survey, the median AI researcher estimated that AGI would arrive in 2040-2060
  • • AI Safety investments grew from US$ 50M in 2020 to US$ 2B+ in 2025
  • • OpenAI, Anthropic, and DeepMind invest 20-30% of their resources in AI safety
  • • The debate over AGI regulation already involves governments from more than 30 countries

✅ Do

  • • Keep up with advances in AI Safety
  • • Participate in governance discussions
  • • Develop critical thinking about AI
  • • Prepare for rapidly changing scenarios

❌ Avoid

  • • Panic over apocalyptic predictions
  • • Completely ignore long-term risks
  • • Believing in highly precise timelines
  • • Confusing current AI with AGI

🏠 Remote Work and AI

The new professional reality

The pandemic accelerated remote work, and AI is taking it to the next level. AI tools are transforming meetings, collaboration, project management, and individual productivity in ways that were unthinkable just a few years ago. The professional of the future won’t be the one who works the most hours, but the one who knows how to use AI to multiply their impact from anywhere in the world.

Main Concept

The AI-powered "digital workplace" operates across four dimensions: (1) Smart communication — AI that summarizes meetings, translates in real time, generates minutes, and identifies action items automatically; (2) Enhanced collaboration — AI copilots built into documents, spreadsheets, and presentations that suggest content, correct errors, and complete tasks; (3) Autonomous management — AI that schedules, prioritizes, monitors projects, and automatically alerts you to risks; (4) Personal productivity — assistants that learn your work patterns and optimize your routine. The "augmented worker" (human + AI) will become the norm, not the exception. Professionals who master AI tools can produce 2–5x more than colleagues who don't use them.

📊 Important Data

  • • 73% of remote workers already use some AI tool daily
  • • AI applied to meetings saves an average of 5 hours per week per professional
  • • Companies with “AI-augmented” workers report 40% higher productivity
  • • The AI productivity tools market is expected to reach US$ 47B by 2028

💡 Practical Tip

Build your "AI productivity stack": (1) Writing assistant (Claude, ChatGPT) for emails, documents, and analysis; (2) Meeting summarization tool (Otter.ai, Fireflies); (3) Repetitive task automation (Zapier, Make); (4) AI-powered personal organization (Notion AI, Todoist). Start with one tool, master it in two weeks, and add the next one.

🎓 Education of the Future

Continuous learning with AI

Education is undergoing its greatest transformation in centuries. The model of “learning for 20 years and working for 40” is being replaced by continuous learning throughout life. AI is both the reason for this need (skills become obsolete faster) and the solution (AI tools personalize and accelerate learning in ways that were once impossible).

Main Concept

The future of education is defined by four pillars: (1) Personalization—AI tutors that adapt content, pace, and style to each student. Instead of one lesson for 30 students, 30 unique learning experiences; (2) Active learning—AI that generates exercises, simulations, and hands-on projects on demand, turning passive consumption into active practice; (3) Skills over content—the focus shifts from memorizing information to developing critical thinking, creativity, problem-solving, and the ability to work with AI; (4) Lifelong learning—micro-courses, certifications, and just-in-time learning that let people continuously update their skills throughout their careers. The most valuable skills of the future will be those AI can’t easily replicate: empathy, original creativity, ethical judgment, and leadership.

💡 Practical Tip

Use AI as your personal tutor: ask Claude or ChatGPT to explain complex concepts in different ways until you understand, generate practice exercises on any subject, and create personalized study plans. The 70-20-10 rule adapted for AI: 70% practice with tools, 20% structured learning (courses), 10% reflection and networking.

✅ Do

  • • Set aside time each week for learning
  • • Use AI as a study tool
  • • Focus on skills that complement AI
  • • Practice active, not passive, learning

❌ Avoid

  • • Thinking a degree is enough for your career
  • • Accumulating certifications without practical application
  • • Memorize content that AI can retrieve
  • • Study only technical skills

🌐 Digital Economy

New Business Models

AI is creating a new economy with business models that didn’t exist two years ago. Companies that sell access to AI models (AI-as-a-Service), creators who monetize AI-generated content, and startups that automate entire sectors with few employees—the rules of the economic game are fundamentally changing. Those who understand these new dynamics can seize enormous opportunities.

Main Concept

The AI-powered digital economy creates opportunities across several layers: (1) Infrastructure layer—companies that provide computing power, data, and foundational models (NVIDIA, cloud providers, OpenAI, Anthropic); (2) Platform layer—marketplaces and tools that let people use AI without technical expertise (Hugging Face, Replicate); (3) Application layer—vertical solutions for specific sectors (AI for healthcare, legal, and finance); (4) Services layer—consulting, training, and implementation; (5) Creator economy—individuals who use AI to create and monetize content, services, and digital products. The “AI solopreneur” model lets one person run businesses that once required teams of 10–20 people.

📊 Important Data

  • • The global generative AI market is expected to reach US$ 1.3 trillion by 2032
  • • AI startups raised US$ 50+ billion in venture capital in 2024
  • • 44% of freelancers already use AI to deliver services faster and with higher quality
  • • “AI-native” companies grow 2-3x faster than traditional competitors

💡 Practical Tip

Identify a specific problem in a niche you know well and solve it with AI. The biggest opportunities lie at the intersection of domain expertise (healthcare, law, education, finance) and AI. You don’t need to be a programmer—no-code AI tools let you create powerful solutions without writing a single line of code.

🧬 Technological Convergence

AI, biotech, quantum, and beyond

The biggest revolutions of the next decade will come not from a single technology, but from the convergence of several. When AI combines with biotechnology, quantum computing, advanced robotics, and nanotechnology, the possibilities multiply exponentially. This convergence is creating solutions to problems that once seemed unsolvable and opening entirely new frontiers for humanity.

Main Concept

Technological convergence operates at powerful intersections: (1) AI + Biotechnology—drug discovery accelerated from 10 years to months, personalized medicine based on genomics, and early disease diagnosis. DeepMind’s AlphaFold revolutionized biology by predicting protein structures; (2) AI + Quantum Computing—quantum computers will solve problems that would take billions of years on classical computers, enabling AI for optimization, molecular simulation, and cryptography; (3) AI + Robotics—autonomous robots in logistics, manufacturing, surgery, and space exploration; (4) AI + IoT—billions of connected sensors generating data that AI processes in real time for smart cities, precision agriculture, and Industry 4.0; (5) AI + Extended Reality—intelligent immersive experiences in training, education, and entertainment.

📊 Important Data

  • • AI accelerated drug discovery—the first drug designed by AI entered clinical trials in 2023
  • • The quantum computing market is expected to grow from US$ 1.3B in 2024 to US$ 12B in 2032
  • • AI-powered autonomous robots already represent a US$ 30B market, growing 25% per year
  • • AI-powered precision agriculture can increase global production by 20% with 30% fewer resources

✅ Do

  • • Keep up with advances in converging technologies
  • • Identify relevant intersections for your sector
  • • Develop interdisciplinary thinking
  • • Build a network across multiple technology fields

❌ Avoid

  • • Focus on just one technology
  • • Disregarding the ethical impacts of convergence
  • • Invest in hype without scientific foundation
  • • Ignore the risks of combined technologies
